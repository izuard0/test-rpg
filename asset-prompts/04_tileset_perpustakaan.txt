=====================================================================
 04 · TILESET PERPUSTAKAAN DIGITAL (BELAJAR MANDIRI)
=====================================================================
Konsep: perpustakaan klasik yang "diperbarui" — rak buku kayu tetap ada
(suasana tenang untuk belajar), tetapi tiap rak punya PANEL LAYAR kecil,
dan ada TERMINAL komputer untuk membuka materi HTML.

Cara kerja di game:
  Pemain mendekati rak buku / terminal -> benda menyala oranye ->
  tekan tombol / ketuk -> muncul panel berisi daftar materi HTML
  (misalnya "Minggu 10 · Pseudocode Lanjut", "Supervised Learning").

Setiap mata kuliah punya rak dengan warna punggung buku berbeda:
  - Logika Komputasional -> buku biru
  - Kecerdasan Buatan    -> buku oranye
  - (cadangan)           -> buku hijau, buku ungu-abu

Perkiraan ukuran peta: 30 x 22 petak.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 4.1 — RAK BUKU (PALING PENTING)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
[blok gaya] Library bookshelf sprites for a computer science faculty, top-down
3/4 view, tall wooden bookshelves full of thick programming textbooks, with a
small glowing cyan display strip on the top of each shelf (blank, no text),
each shelf 64x64 pixels (2 tiles wide, 2 tiles tall). Make 4 color variants
where the book spines are mostly: blue, orange, green, slate purple-grey.
For EACH variant also make a highlighted version with a soft glowing orange
outline (#E8833A) and small sparkles, used when the player is near.
Also: a short bookshelf (64x32), an end-of-row shelf seen from the side
(32x64), and a shelf with a few books missing.
